Sharing revenue with creators

X.LA served creators who owned intellectual property. Its public announcement described sharing revenue when their work was used or licensed. My part was translating commercial models into the product roadmap.

How we tested checkout at Xsolla R&D

In a separate R&D experiment, I defined the hypothesis and metrics for a 21-day A/B test. The variant combined regional payment methods with a shorter form. Successful-payment conversion was 48% higher than control, ARPU was 6% higher than control, and chargebacks remained stable.

How we tested hypotheses

I ran 30 A/B tests in GrowthBook across early-stage R&D products, with approximately 5,000 users per test. The checkout test compared a complete checkout variant, so its result applies to the two changes together.

Retention programmes in Southeast Asia

The R&D team localised retention programmes for Southeast Asian markets. Customer lifetime value (LTV) rose 36% and acquisition cost (CAC) fell 27%.

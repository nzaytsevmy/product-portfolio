Comparing loan terms

Visitors compare loan amounts, durations and costs. A lender profile adds terms and reviews before someone follows an offer. The Finlove example shows how terms differ for first-time and repeat borrowers.

Why the terms are presented separately

A lender may offer different durations and costs to first-time and repeat borrowers. The profile presents these scenarios separately so visitors can check the relevant terms before continuing to an application.

How I would analyse the product

Can people identify the terms that apply to them?

What to test on a lender profile

I’d show lender profiles to first-time borrowers and people who have used that lender before. In each group, I’d ask participants to identify the relevant amount, duration and cost, then find where to apply. The share who correctly identify their own terms would show where the profile blurs the two offers.

What to monitor after a change

I’d compare two ways of presenting the terms. If the lender reports application status, I’d measure the share of profile visitors whose application was accepted for review. If only outbound clicks are available, I’d pair click measurement with a comprehension test. The click records the handoff to the lender.

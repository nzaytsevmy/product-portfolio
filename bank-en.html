Choosing a banking product in Kazakhstan

I lead product development for Bank.kz. One of its core journeys helps people compare what different banks offer.

My roleProduct lead
Explore depositsDo the filters help people choose a suitable deposit?

Interest rates and access to funds

Visitors start by choosing a currency and deposit type. These filters narrow the catalogue to relevant products before they compare individual terms.

How to compare two deposits

A visitor can compare deposits in the same currency and look at both interest rates and access to funds. For someone who may need to withdraw part of the balance early, the access terms matter alongside the rate.

How I would analyse the product

Do the filters help people choose a suitable deposit?

What makes a deposit suitable?

I’d compare the current list with a version that puts withdrawal conditions beside the rate. In a usability test, I’d ask participants to find a deposit in a specified currency that allows early partial withdrawal. I’d measure the share who choose a suitable deposit and the time it takes.

How to monitor catalogue quality

In the live funnel, I’d measure how often selecting a currency and deposit type leaves an empty list. If this happens frequently, I’d check catalogue coverage and the filters before changing the sort order. For listed offers, I’d verify the rate and withdrawal terms against the bank’s information.
